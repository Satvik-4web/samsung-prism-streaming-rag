# Samsung PRISM Program

## Section 1: Overview
The Samsung PRISM program is a prestigious industry-academia initiative that partners engineering students with Samsung R&D Institute India to work on real-world advanced technology projects. 

## Section 2: Theme 4 Requirements
Samsung PRISM Theme 4 specifically focuses on Advanced Conversational AI. It requires the development of a Streaming Live RAG architecture that can process voice and text incrementally, ensuring low latency. 

## Section 3: Engineering Benchmarks
The key requirements for the Samsung PRISM project include a sub-500 millisecond latency budget, real-time multi-intent extraction, delta refinement for follow-up queries, and a robust deterministic verification engine to prevent LLM hallucinations. For final project submissions, teams must provide an interactive frontend demo that proves the backend architectural requirements are met.
