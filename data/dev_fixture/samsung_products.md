## Section 4: Troubleshooting - Battery Drain & Overheating
If your Samsung Galaxy device is overheating or experiencing battery drain:
- Stop Intensive Use: Close all apps and let it cool down in a well-ventilated area.
- Power Saving Mode: Enable this in Battery settings to limit background sync and processing.
- Background Apps: Use 'Put unused apps to sleep' in Background usage limits.
- Light Performance Profile: Switch to this profile to reduce heat and improve battery efficiency.
- Updates: Always install the latest software updates to patch known bugs.
- Diagnostics: Run the Phone Diagnostics tool in the Samsung Members app to check battery health.
